Sicong Pan (潘思聪)

I am a Ph.D. student in the Humanoid Robots Lab at the University of Bonn under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Maren Bennewitz. I received my M.S. degree from Fudan University under the supervision of Prof. Hui Wei. My research focuses on active perception in tabletop and agricultural environments, with an emphasis on view planning and 3D object reconstruction.
